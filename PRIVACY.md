# Privacy Notice

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Health Timeline is a demonstration that displays two synthetic laboratory reports. The checked-in reports and all values shown by the demo are fictional and are not real people's health information.

## Local demo

When run as documented with `npm start`, the server binds to `127.0.0.1` and serves the included static files and synthetic timeline from the user's computer. The application code in this repository does not collect visitor data, provide report uploads or accounts, run analytics or advertising, or connect to Cerebras or another external data service. It does not send entered health information to a model. The demo has no health-data entry feature.

The local server writes errors to its process console. The application itself does not implement request logging or persistent visitor-data storage. The computer, operating system, browser, network, or tools used to run or access the demo may have their own logs and data practices.

## Public or hosted deployments

This repository describes a local demo. It does not establish the data practices of any public deployment, hackathon platform, reverse proxy, hosting provider, analytics service, or model provider. Those services may collect connection data or logs under their own terms. The local demo does not control or take responsibility for how a separately selected AI model provider handles data or generates responses. Before making a hosted or AI-connected version available, its operator should identify the services involved and explain what data they receive, how they use it, how long they retain it, and how users can contact the operator about privacy questions. Do not submit real personal or health information to a hosted or AI-connected version unless its operator has published and implemented appropriate privacy and security practices.

AI models can produce inaccurate, incomplete, or fabricated information (“hallucinations”). Check AI-generated information against reliable sources. Do not rely on model output for medical decisions, and consult a qualified healthcare professional about personal health questions.

## Medical use

This project is for demonstration and educational purposes only and uses synthetic data. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and is not a substitute for advice from a qualified healthcare professional. Do not use it to make decisions about your health.

## Contact

For privacy questions about a particular deployment, contact the person or organization operating that deployment.
